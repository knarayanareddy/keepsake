A 16×16 arena where the rules are enforced in bytecode, and the only thing the bytecode is willing to certify is a choice that cost you something.

2contracts, 11,120 B runtime

Folio 01 · the problemthree gaps

A ledger records what happened to balances.
Not what you chose.

  1. i.

    Merit has no place to live

    In any competitive system the interesting fact is a restrained action: you had the shot and did not take it. State says hp stayed at 2 — it cannot say why. Everything that answers "who was generous under pressure" today lives in someone's application database, and dies with it.

  2. ii.

    Attestation has an issuer problem

    You can sign a claim about yourself — which makes it worthless. Or you coordinate a registry to sign it for you — which costs a key, a policy, a review queue and a trust relationship. Both failure modes come from the same assumption: that a fact needs an authority.

  3. iii.

    The record expires with the product

    Rounds reset, servers stop, hackathon demos go back to localhost. A fact you can only read through the service that produced it is not a fact — it is a customer relationship.

So: put the claim about a choice in the one place that already enforces the rules of that choice — and make lying impossible rather than merely discouraged.

Folio 02 · the ideaone sentence, then the mechanism

Make the referee the only witness,
and make testimony cost something.

  • The World is the only attester. HonorLog accepts writes from exactly one address, pinned once at deploy. There is no function that lets you certify yourself — not a permission we withheld, a function that does not exist.
  • A record can only mint alongside an action the rules already checked. You cannot attest a mercy while your opponent is healthy; the guard reverts first.
  • A betrayal carries the id of the trust it broke. refUID points at your own pact, so one read proves both halves.
  • The record is a soulbound token, not a row. It outlives the match, the round, the app.

gilt rule = a pact that stands · dashed wax = the betrayal the log kept, after the board forgot

Deliberately absent: a kill that mints an achievement, a token, a prize pool, an admin call, a front end that is the source of truth.

The whole record is nine words wide, and it is checkable, which a leaderboard is not.

// contracts/World.sol — the guard that makes the mercy mean something
function spare(address other) external {
    … if (p.ammo == 0) revert NoAmmo();
    if (o.hp > SPARE_WINDOW || o.ammo == 0) revert NotKillShot();
    uid = log.attest(… kind = SPARE …);   // the World is the only caller
  }
// a kill: hp → 0, alive → false. no attest(). no credit. by design.
  • 2 hp and still armed is what "I could have" means on-chain.
  • Full health, click Spare → NotKillShot(). The chain refuses to flatter you.

16×16 field · 5 hp · 6 rounds · SPARE_WINDOW = 2 hp · both contracts are EIP-170-compliant at 3,425 B and 7,695 B of runtime.

Folio 04 · the artifactwhat one fact costs, and what it holds

An attestation, deliberately EAS-shaped,
with the contract as its registry.

uid
keccak over schema, world, subject, other, matchId, block, refUID, kind, a nonce
schema
one per kind — PACT · SPARE · BETRAYAL · CONDUCT
attester
the World. always. verify() returns it, so provenance is checkable, not assumed
subject
the actor — the one who spent the shot or the mercy
other
the counterparty, so a record names both sides
refUID
for a betrayal: the exact pact it broke
matchId
stamped at mint, so a pact never dangles into a later round
token
soulbound ERC-721: it outlives the match, and cannot be traded
revoke
an honoured pact loses its credit if it ends in betrayal — you cannot claim both

Nothing about this is a signed promise from us. It is a view function anyone can call, forever:

HonorLog.verify(bytes32 uid)
  → (bool ok, address attester, uint8 kind,
     address subject, address other, bytes32 refUID)

ok = true   attester = 0xWorld…   kind = BETRAYAL
  • Unpredictable by design. The trailing nonce means you cannot compute your own uid before the tx — you read it from Attested. That is why the UI's docket is load-bearing, not decorative.
  • No oracle, no relayer, no indexer required to read it back.

ALICE spares BOB — the kill shot was real, 2 hp and still armed — so the chain mints it. Then she shoots him, and the log writes the betrayal against the id of the pledge she just made.

Then a new match opens: BOB re-enters at (1,13), every score back to zero. Four records still resolve for ALICE, and verify(betrayal) still returns ok = true, attester = the World.

This match was executed against the patched bytecode in a local EVM (solc 0.8.24, shanghai, optimizer 200) and serialized frame by frame into web/demo-match.json. It is a recording, and the page says so in its header; the numbers are the ones the hero image and the card quote. One receipt on testnet is the deploy-day check.

Folio 06 · the guaranteesand the limits, stated

Not "trustless". Just fewer doors.

  • Write-once pin. setWorld() cannot be re-pointed (WorldAlreadySet), and attest() has one allowed caller.
  • No self-certification. The subject is msg.sender of a World call; the World is the only wallet the log answers.
  • Match-scoped pacts. A shot is only a betrayal if the pact was made in this match.
  • Credit is not additive. Break the pact you were honoured for and the kept-count comes back.
  • Board rotation is owner-gated, so nobody on a public testnet can freeze every live player.
  • A plain kill is not recorded. Only a betrayed pact mints. We could add a KILL kind; we chose not to, because an achievement is not a fact about character.
  • The owner can rotate the match (and HonorLog.renounceOwner() can freeze the attester pin permanently — after the demo, not before).
  • There is no revocation of a true record and no privacy layer: everything is public, and that is the point.
  • Gameplay is not the contribution. A 16×16 arena is a demonstration surface for one primitive: attestation by constraint.

adjacency is a precondition of every verb: you cannot owe a mercy at a distance

Folio 07 · the chainthe property we actually exercise

On a 12-second chain this is a form submission.
On a 300-millisecond one it is a moment.

  • The attested write lands in the same breath as the move. A mercy you have to wait four blocks to confirm is a tweet; one that paints inside the next block is a fact.
  • Non-conflicting parallel writes. Two players touch different storage slots, so both mash move and both land. The conflict that does replay — the shot at the shared hp — is the game.
  • Gas is a limit, not a guess. Monad charges gas_limit, so the UI sends tight per-verb caps (45k–420k, measured cold + 20%) instead of letting a wallet pad it.
  • Small state, honestly sized. 11,120 bytes of runtime, 14 typed errors, no proxy, no framework — the whole thing is auditable in one sitting.

Folio 08 · state of playwhat is built, what is a deploy

Everything except the transaction.

  • Contracts: patched guards, write-once attester, match-scoped pacts, revocable credit, 16 Foundry tests, 27 runtime probes written against the compiled bytecode.
  • Front end: one HTML file, ethers and the typefaces vendored, no build step, no CDN. Boots read-only over the public RPC with no wallet; a spectator sees both players from Spawned/Moved/Shot plus a bounded log scan.
  • Nothing configured? it plays the recorded match and labels itself: "nothing signed, nothing spent". The fallback is the demo, not a compromise.
  • Interface: a canal-city notary's register — paper chrome, night playfield, square seals, a dated HonorLog docket, radius 0. Encoded as 46 machine checks, because a design rule nobody verifies decays.
  • Deck, card copy, deploy runbook, submission sheet — SUBMISSION.md, DEPLOY.md, DEEPDIVE.md with the audit trail.

Folio 09 · try it yourself60 seconds, no account with us

The demo is deliberately adversarial to a pitch deck.

  1. 1.

    Open the Live URL

    Two wallet tabs, two browser profiles, or one laptop and a phone. Connect adds Monad testnet if your wallet is elsewhere; the page refuses mainnet out loud.

  2. 2.

    Both spawn

    Coordinates come from block entropy, so neither of you chooses where you land. Within ~2 s each board shows the other — or paste their address into Watch.

  3. 3.

    Try to fake a mercy

    Click Spare on a healthy neighbour: NotKillShot. That refusal is the product.

  4. 4.

    Pact → wound → Spare → Shoot

    The docket gains a BETRAYAL whose refUID is your own pact. Click its uid to call verify() in the page.

  5. 5.

    Then rotate the match

    Board cleared, scores zero — the four records still resolve. Read them from the explorer's Read tab instead of from us.
